The Foundation
Built at 4:00 AM
If you told me a year ago that waking up before the sun—when the rest of the world is dead quiet and pitch black outside—would become my absolute favorite part of the day, I’d have thought you were out of your mind. But that’s the thing about this past year. It wasn't made of grand, sweeping movie moments. It was built at 4:00 AM.
Every Morning
The Quiet Haze
It was built in that half-asleep haze, reaching for the phone just to hear your voice, to see your smile, and to watch you head off into your day while I sat in the quiet of mine.
Across The Miles
Our Sanctuary
We’ve spent three hundred and sixty-five days figuring out how to belong to each other across the miles. We built our own little sanctuary out of shared playlists, late-night songs played at the piano, digital pages of stories we wrote together, and a thousand tiny running jokes that make zero sense to anyone else on earth. We learned each other's rhythms—when to laugh, when to listen, and how to make a screen feel like a warm, crowded room.
Day 365
Proof & Intention
Reaching a year together isn't just a number on a calendar for us. It’s proof. Proof that distance is just geography when two people are this intentional about choosing each other every single day. It’s the pride of looking at everything we’ve grown through, every routine we’ve built, and every personal milestone we’ve cheered each other on for.
Looking Forward
My Favorite Plot Twist
A year in, and you still feel like my favorite plot twist. I love who we are, I love what we’ve built, and I can’t wait for every single morning that comes next.
